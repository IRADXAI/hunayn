Why "Hunayn"?

Hunayn ibn Ishaq (809–873) was the greatest translator of Baghdad's House of Wisdom and author of the first systematic treatise on the eye. He turned raw knowledge into precise, trusted text, and was famously paid in gold by the weight of his manuscripts, because accuracy was the product.

That is the job of a radiology report: seeing clearly, then translating what you saw into words a clinician can act on. Hunayn the tool does the translation. The seeing stays yours.

Built by a practicing radiologist who got tired of typing normals.
